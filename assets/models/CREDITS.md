# Credits

## Player model

This work is based on "American Football Player" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/american-football-player-10bf41a1c05a4c9cb74676b7dd8cb18e) by blakeallen25, licensed under CC-BY-4.0.

Changes: reduced from about 479,000 to about 5,600 triangles, hidden geometry removed, helmet shell rebuilt, shoes replaced with simple cleats, skeleton trimmed to 26 Mixamo bones, uniform textures blanked of all team names, numbers and brand logos so they can be repainted.

## Animation

- Universal Animation Library (Standard) by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0. Used for idle, stance, walk, jog, sprint, block, hit, fall, roll and jump, retargeted onto the Mixamo skeleton.
- Rokoko free sports animation pack, "Football_Quarterback_mixamo.fbx". Used for qb_throw. Free clips from Rokoko (https://www.rokoko.com).
